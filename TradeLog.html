Fidelity Export
✅ Use the file that starts with History_for_Account… — that's the one with your actual trades in it.
1
On Fidelity.com, open the Activity & Orders tab
2
Select a single account, then the History sub-tab and your date range
3
Click Download → saves History_for_Account_X…….csv
4
Drop it in as-is — dividends, reinvestments, journal entries and cash sweeps are filtered out automatically, and expired options are closed at $0
⛔ Not Portfolio_Positions_<date>.csv. That one is a snapshot of what you currently hold — no buys, sells or trade dates, so there is nothing to journal. TradeLogger will tell you if you drop it in by mistake.
⚠️ Fidelity's history export has no execution times — only a Run Date. P&L, win rate and the calendar are exact, but hold durations and the P&L by Hour chart aren't meaningful for these trades. Active Trader Pro exports that include a Time column are used as-is.
